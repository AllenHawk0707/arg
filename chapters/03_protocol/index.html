maps to a 5.7 Hz inverted phase signal — but we realized too late
that "the signal" was never received. It was GENERATED. By us.
The 5.7 Hz pattern is the resonance of OUR OWN equipment playing
to itself, scaled up. The 91 symbols were the INPUT we feed the
resonator. We were the signature all along.

— POL, 2023.06.13 23:50 local (last entry before silence)
